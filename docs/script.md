## Recording conditions

- For a simulated raid, say **“This raid is simulated for rehearsal”** immediately before that segment; trim the same number of words elsewhere. Keep the caption visible throughout. A genuine raid is preferable but is not required to show authentic chat integration.
- Use a consenting second account/person for any claim that “a viewer” submitted a message. If Shivam types it himself, say “I type” instead. Do not imply independent users or customers.
- If the actual product does not automatically place accepted text into the brief, demonstrate the creator editing/publishing it; do not edit footage to suggest automation.
- Keep account secrets, access tokens and private profile information outside the frame. Use original visuals and no unlicensed soundtrack.
- Show the Codex thread briefly but legibly. Replace the final repository URL card only after the URL is real.
- Upload to YouTube as **Public**, not Unlisted. Check the URL while signed out.

## Capture checklist

1. Prepare a brief with enough time left for the recording; rehearse the closing/expiry shot separately if necessary.
2. Connect Twitch and verify real chat events reach the app before recording.
3. Arrange the creator desk, Twitch chat, public view and OBS view for readable cuts; capture at 1080p or higher.
4. Record one clean product run, then the Codex cutaway. Preserve enough continuous footage to substantiate the command-to-update sequence.
5. Record narration at an even pace. Do not accelerate the real-time interaction so much that it obscures latency.
6. Review the final cut for truthfulness, readable text, audible speech and a duration below 3:00.
